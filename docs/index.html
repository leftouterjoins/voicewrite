How It Works

1

Launch

VoiceWrite lives in your menu bar. Grant microphone and accessibility permissions on first run.

2

Hit Ctrl+V (customizable) from anywhere. The screen border lights up to show you're recording.

3

Speak

Talk naturally. See your words appear in real-time with live transcription preview.

4

Done

Press the hotkey again. Your transcribed text is typed into the active app automatically.

FAQ

How is VoiceWrite different from other dictation apps?

VoiceWrite is designed for quick, single-line input — not long-form dictation. It's ideal for form fields, quick messages, search queries, short notes, and vibe coding. For paragraphs or documents, use macOS's built-in Dictation.

Is VoiceWrite a good Wispr Flow alternative?

Yes! VoiceWrite offers similar core features—real-time voice typing, hotkey activation, multi-language support, and smart punctuation—but completely free and with better privacy since everything runs on-device.

Why choose VoiceWrite over paid voice dictation apps?

VoiceWrite is free, open source, and processes all audio locally on your Mac. No subscription fees, no cloud uploads, no account required. Your voice data never leaves your computer.

How does the live preview work?

VoiceWrite shows your transcription in a floating preview window as you speak. When you stop recording, the final text is pasted into your active app. This gives you a chance to see what was transcribed before it's inserted.

Does it work in Terminal?

Yes! VoiceWrite pastes text using Cmd+V which works in Terminal and virtually any app. For the best experience, enable the VoiceWrite Input Method in System Settings.
